# Unknown-material challenge and software QA

At the previously fixed correlation threshold 0.9652137650222872, 502/508 non-PP/PVC spectra were rejected (98.82%); 6/508 were accepted (1.18%). Three of 58 nylon fragment spectra and three of 55 PS spectra were falsely accepted under provisional folder labels; the remaining 395 spectra across 14 material classes were rejected. These data come from the same published experimental collection, not an independent seaweed field dataset. No threshold was retuned. Near-duplicate spectra, label provenance and specimen independence still need investigation. The 8 screening unit tests passed locally, including rejection of flat, nonfinite, duplicate-axis, truncated and sparse spectra. Streamlit browser testing and deployment not performed. Direct comparison with the official Open Specy library remains outstanding; the correlation baseline is not an official Open Specy execution. Reference library conversion and multiclass validation remain research tasks.
